# Illinois CPS Discovery — Questions to Investigate

**Status:** Living document. A structured question set for the Illinois CPS
operation visit. The goal is to replace assumptions (see
[assumptions.md](./assumptions.md)) and answer [open-questions.md](./open-questions.md)
with observed reality. Capture answers inline and update the assumptions/open
questions afterward.

---

## Current Workflow
- How do equipment requests arrive today (phone, email, form, in person)?
- How are requests tracked from intake to delivery?
- Who touches a request between submission and fulfillment?
- What information is required before a request can be acted on?
- Where do requests get "stuck" today?

## Asset Tracking
- How is equipment tracked today (spreadsheet, software, paper, tags)?
- Is there a single source of truth for "where is asset X right now"?
- How is availability determined before committing equipment?
- How are serialized vs bulk/consumable items handled?

## Equipment Classification
- How does CPS categorize its fleet (categories, classes, families)?
- What attributes matter for finding the right machine (size, capacity, fuel)?
- Are accessories/attachments tracked separately or with the parent?

## Numbering
- What asset tag / ID format is used today?
- Is the number tied to the machine for life, or reassigned?
- How is history kept when a machine is repaired/rebuilt/retired?

## Job / Project Coding
- How are jobs/projects identified in CPS today?
- Where do cost codes / phase codes come from?
- Who owns project setup and access?
- How are addresses/locations for delivery determined?

## Transfers
- How are transfers between projects handled today?
- Who authorizes a transfer, and how is it recorded?
- How is billing/cost affected by a transfer?

## Transportation
- Who delivers equipment (CPS drivers, third party, mixed)?
- What DOT requirements apply?
- How are delivery/pickup charges calculated and recorded?
- How is proof of delivery/pickup captured?

## Returns & Inspections
- What is the return process today, step by step?
- Is there a standard inspection checklist? Can we get a copy?
- How is condition/damage documented (photos, forms)?
- What event actually stops billing?

## Maintenance
- Who performs maintenance (in-house, vendor, both)?
- How is maintenance tracked and scheduled?
- Are PM schedules based on hours, calendar, or both?
- How are meter readings collected?

## External Rentals
- When does CPS rent from outside vendors?
- Who orders external rentals, and with what authority?
- Which vendors are preferred, and why?
- How is an external rental reconciled against a request/project?

## Billing & Rates
- How are rates set today (internal transfer vs external)?
- When does billing start and stop?
- How are charges applied to a project/job?
- How are overtime/extra usage handled?

## Reporting
- What reports does CPS produce today?
- What reports does CPS wish it had?
- Who consumes these reports, and how often?

## Technology
- What software/spreadsheets are used today?
- What are the biggest technology pain points?
- What works well that we should not break?
- What devices are used in the field (phone, tablet, laptop)?

## Preferred Vendors
- Who are the go-to vendors for equipment CPS doesn't own?
- Are there contracts/negotiated rates in place?

## Staffing
- What roles exist in the Illinois CPS operation?
- Who does what across intake, fulfillment, logistics, maintenance, billing?
- Where are the coverage gaps or single points of failure?

## Florida Comparison
- How does the Florida CPS operation differ from Illinois?
- Are processes, roles, and systems the same or divergent?
- What should be common vs region-specific in a future system?
